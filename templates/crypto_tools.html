🔤 Vigenere

Cipher substitusi polyalphabetik klasik. Beroperasi pada ASCII printable (32–126). Kunci di-cycling per karakter.

🔲 Playfair

Cipher digram 5×5. Hanya huruf A–Z (I=J). Karakter non-huruf dilewati.

⚡ ChaCha20

Stream cipher modern. Nonce 16-byte acak. Output: nonce_hex:ciphertext_b64.

🐡 Blowfish-CBC

Block cipher CBC mode, PKCS#7 padding. IV 8-byte acak. Output: iv_hex:ct_b64.
